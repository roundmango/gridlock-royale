HOW TO PLAY

Win

Be the last car standing. Everyone gets one respawn; lose both lives and you're out. The storm shrinks the island in six phases, and outside the ring it eats your health.

Touch controls

Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen and your car drives toward your thumb. Point backward to pull a skidding U-turn. On the right: FIRE shoots your selected weapon, MG holds the machine guns (they also fire on their own at anything in your sights), BOOST burns nitro, and SPECIAL fires your ride's ability when it's charged. Tap the weapon squares to switch.

Keyboard

Arrows or WASD drive · Space fire · F machine guns · Shift boost · E special · Q / Tab switch weapon · 1–5 pick a slot · C handbrake · M map · Esc pause.

Heat

Wrecking traffic, killing drivers and shooting cops raises your wanted level. One star brings cruisers, three brings SWAT and a chopper, five brings tanks and a gunship. Lose them by hiding, use a bribe, or roll slowly into a RESPRAY garage (green chevrons) for a fresh paint job. Wrecking a 3★+ driver pays a bounty.

Loot

Weapon crates glow in the weapon's color. Green crates repair, blue canisters refill nitro, gold kits upgrade, cash stacks bribe the cops. Airdrops with a light pillar land inside the circle: they carry super weapons like the orbital laser, an airstrike, or a tactical nuke.
